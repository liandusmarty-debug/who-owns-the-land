# Request: reuse of the People's Land Map public-land parcels (Ndifuna Ukwazi)

**To:** Ndifuna Ukwazi, via the contact page at https://nu.org.za
**Subject:** Collaboration request: reusing People's Land Map parcels in an open neighbourhood map

---

Dear Ndifuna Ukwazi team,

I'm writing on behalf of *Who Owns the Land?*, a non-profit open-data project that profiles Cape Town
neighbourhoods. We show living conditions (from the census) next to who owns the land underneath them.
The work is area-level only, and our code and methods are public at
https://github.com/liandusmarty-debug/who-owns-the-land.

The People's Land Map is the most thorough public record of state-owned land in Cape Town that we've
found. We know the City refused your requests for its land list, so we know how much work went into it.

We'd like to ask whether we may **reuse the parcel identifiers and owner type (City, Province,
National, other state entity) from the People's Land Map**, to mark known public land on our map.
We would:

- credit Ndifuna Ukwazi and link to the People's Land Map wherever the data appears;
- use only parcel IDs and owner *type*, with no other attributes unless you agree;
- share back any corrections we find; and
- follow any conditions you set, including non-commercial use.

We'd also be glad to hear any thoughts on the Masiphumelele area, which is our first in-depth pilot.
