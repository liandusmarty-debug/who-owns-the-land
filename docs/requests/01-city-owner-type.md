# Request: owner category per land parcel (City of Cape Town)

**To:** open.data@capetown.gov.za. Cc: city.maps@capetown.gov.za
**Subject:** Open data request: owner category (not owner names) for Land Parcels dataset

---

Dear Open Data team,

I'm writing on behalf of *Who Owns the Land?*, a non-profit project that publishes neutral,
neighbourhood-level information about land ownership and living conditions in Cape Town. The aim is
to help residents understand the land around them. Our code and methods are public at
https://github.com/liandusmarty-debug/who-owns-the-land.

We already use the City's **Land Parcels** open dataset (Property FeatureServer). It has no owner
information. We are asking for **one additional attribute: the owner category of each parcel**,
keyed by `SG26_CODE` or `PRTY_NMBR`, using broad categories such as:

> City of Cape Town · Western Cape Government · National Government / state entity ·
> Private · Other (e.g. trust, church, community property association)

**We are not asking for owner names or any personal information.** The categories describe
landholders, not individuals, and private owners would only be shown as "Private". This is in line
with POPIA and with section 7 of the City's Open Data Policy (27781), which favours releasing
non-personal data.

If a full dataset isn't possible, any of these would help:
1. owner category for parcels in the **Masiphumelele and Noordhoek** area (our pilot); or
2. a list of **City-owned parcels** (SG26 codes only).

We will credit the City of Cape Town Open Data Portal on everything we publish. If this is better
handled as a PAIA request, please let me know and we'll submit one.
