## Third-party data

The outputs are derived from third-party datasets (Statistics South Africa, City of Cape Town,
Surveyor-General and others). Those sources stay under their own terms, which are recorded per
dataset in `sources.md`. Our CC BY 4.0 licence covers only our own work on top of them. If a source's
terms are stricter, those terms win for anything derived from that source.

Synthetic sample data in `outputs/_sample/` is fake and must not be presented as real findings.
